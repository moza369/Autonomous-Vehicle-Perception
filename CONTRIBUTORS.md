# Contribution Policy

* Every contributor must work only on their assigned task.
* Every contributor must use their own branch (`feature/teamXX`).
* All contributions must be submitted through a Pull Request.
* Pull Requests are reviewed by the Team Leader before being merged into the `develop` branch.
* Direct commits to `main` or `develop` are not allowed.
